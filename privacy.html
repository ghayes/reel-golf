Last Updated: September 11, 2026

Reel Golf ("we", "us", or "our") operates the web game accessible at app.reel-golf.com. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the minimal personal information you share with us.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum information necessary to authenticate your account and save your gameplay stats:

  • Phone Number: Provided directly by you when signing in or creating an account via SMS one-time passcode (OTP).
  • Game Profile & Gameplay Data: Your chosen public display name, game scores, longest drives, catches, inventory, and unlocked achievements/trophies.
  • Technical Data: Basic browser user-agent and IP address logged temporarily for security and rate-limiting purposes.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used solely for the following purposes:

  • To deliver one-time verification passcodes (OTP) for account login and authentication via SMS.
  • To save your personal game progress, coin balance, and inventory across devices and game sessions.
  • To display your public display name and scores on the global leaderboard and trophy wall.
  • To prevent automated abuse, spam, and toll fraud.

3. SMS & Mobile Information Disclosure (10DLC Compliance)

Strict Mobile Privacy Commitment:
No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All other categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties under any circumstances.

We do not sell, rent, trade, or distribute your phone number or text messaging opt-in status. Mobile numbers are utilized strictly for account authentication and security communications requested by the user.

4. Service Providers & Third Parties

We only share data with essential technical service providers who assist in operating our game services:

  • Database & Authentication Infrastructure: Supabase (secure database hosting and authentication services).
  • SMS Gateway Providers: Sent.dm (used strictly for programmatic delivery of user-requested one-time login passcodes).

These infrastructure providers are contractually obligated to handle data confidentially and are prohibited from using your personal information for any secondary purpose.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

We retain your phone number and associated gameplay stats for as long as your account remains active. You may request permanent deletion of your phone number, account, and all associated stats at any time by emailing us at support@reel-golf.com.

6. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures, including HTTPS encryption in transit, strict database Row Level Security (RLS) policies, and tokenized session management to protect your information against unauthorized access.

7. Children's Privacy

Reel Golf is not directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
