SNAP!

REEL GOLF

Field-tested off a dock · est. sometime in the ’70s
HOLD to wind up your swing. RELEASE to hit.
Land in the red and your line snaps — ball gone.

Then HOLD to reel the ball back. Watch the tension.
If a fish grabs on: reel when it rests, let go when it runs.

Points bank only when the ball reaches the dock.
Rings on the water = 2× that shot. You get 3 balls.
